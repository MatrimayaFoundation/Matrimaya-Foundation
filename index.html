About Matrimaya Foundation

Matrimaya Foundation is an education-focused organization based in Punjab, India. Our intended mission is to help students explore educational opportunities and work towards a future where financial challenges are less of a barrier to learning.

Through our planned scholarship and financial assistance initiatives, we aim to encourage learning, opportunity, and personal development.
